# Agency Management SaaS

A UI/UX case study for a SaaS platform designed to help creative agencies manage projects, tasks, clients, invoices, expenses and business performance from one dashboard.

## Problem

Small agencies often manage projects, clients, finances and team tasks across multiple disconnected tools, making it difficult to understand overall business performance.

## Goal

Design a simple centralized platform where agency owners can manage projects, monitor tasks, track invoices and understand revenue and expenses.

## Core Features

- Project dashboard
- Project management
- Task tracking
- Client management
- Invoice tracking
- Income and expense analytics
- Reports
- Notifications

## Design Process

Research → User Flow → Wireframes → UI Design → Prototype → Testing → Iteration

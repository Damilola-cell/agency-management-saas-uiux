# UX Audit — Agency Management SaaS

## Overview

This UX audit explores common usability challenges faced by small creative agencies when managing projects, clients, tasks, invoices and business performance across multiple tools.

## Key Problems Identified

### 1. Fragmented Workflow
Agency teams often switch between different tools for project management, communication, invoicing and financial tracking.

### 2. Poor Visibility
Important information such as overdue tasks, project progress, unpaid invoices and expenses can be difficult to see at a glance.

### 3. Complex Navigation
Business management platforms can become overwhelming when too many features compete for attention.

### 4. Difficult Financial Tracking
Agency owners need a simple way to understand income, expenses, outstanding invoices and overall financial performance.

## Design Opportunities

- Create a centralized dashboard for important business information.
- Prioritize projects and urgent tasks.
- Make invoice statuses easy to understand.
- Simplify navigation between projects, clients and finances.
- Use clear visual hierarchy and progressive disclosure.
- Provide useful empty states and contextual actions.

## Proposed Solution

Design a centralized SaaS experience that allows agency owners and teams to manage projects, tasks, clients and finances without constantly switching between multiple platforms.
